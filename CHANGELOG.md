# Changelog

All notable changes to the Bitcoin Deepa brand are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and releases use [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/) as described in the README.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Public brand site on GitHub Pages: the brand guidelines and the colour system as web pages, with every asset at a stable URL and downloadable zips (all assets, SVG only, PNG only) built on each change.
- A visual quick reference in the README.

### Changed

- The guideline pages moved from `guidelines/` to `site/` and now load images as files instead of embedding them, so they open quickly.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-20

First release of the brand in this repository.

### Added

- Primary logo in black and white versions, and the primary logo with the *Pearl of Satoshi* slogan.
- Horizontal wordmark, and the wordmark with the ₿ coin.
- The Yaka symbol: gradient, bold, extra bold, single-colour black and white, left- and right-aligned half Yaka, laser-eyed Yaka and the laser flare.
- App icons with the දීප symbol: light, dark and orange, each in standard and large-glyph versions.
- Logos for orange grounds.
- Slogan artwork in four colourways.
- The primary ₿ pattern and the secondary spiky-tile pattern in light and charcoal.
- Profile pictures with the ₿ coin and දීප mark on black, white and Satoshi Orange.
- Brand guidelines (`guidelines/brand.html`) and the colour system (`guidelines/colour.html`).
- Design system: brand book, design tokens as JSON and CSS, light and dark themes taken from the app designs.

### Colour

- Satoshi Orange #F8931A, Genesis Black #000001, Whitepaper White #FFFFFE.
- Grounds: Cold Storage Charcoal #1D1E1D, Paper Wallet Cream #FFFCFA.
- Lanka colours: Perahera Maroon #8D183A, Sinharaja Green #00544F, Vesak Gold #FFBE2B, Thambili Orange #EA7400.
