Bitcoin Deepa is a CeylonCash brand that brings Bitcoin to Sri Lanka. The name pairs the English word *bitcoin* with the Sinhala **දීප** (*deepa*, "island"), and the slogan **Pearl of Satoshi** plays on Sri Lanka's old title, the Pearl of the Indian Ocean. The identity has three parts: a Yaka mask drawn in a flame gradient, the bilingual wordmark, and Bitcoin orange.

## Logo

### Primary logo

The primary logo is the stacked lockup: Yaka mask above the wordmark **bitcoin දීප**. There are two versions, and one of them goes on almost everything.

| File | Use on | Wordmark |
|---|---|---|
| `BDLogo_Black.svg` (Primary Logo) | White and light grounds | "bitcoin" in `brand-black`, දීප in `bitcoin-orange` |
| `BDLogo_White.svg` (Primary Logo) | Black and dark grounds | "bitcoin" in `brand-white`, දීප in `bitcoin-orange` |

- Use the SVG files. Use PNG only where SVG is not accepted.
- The mask is always the `yaka-1` → `yaka-6` gradient line art in full-colour versions. Do not recolour it, fill it or thicken the lines.
- Never retype the wordmark. "bitcoin" and දීප are drawn artwork, and the දීප glyph is custom lettering that no font reproduces.

### Secondary lockups

- **Logo with slogan** (`PrimaryBlack_w_Slogan.png`, `PrimaryWhite_w_Slogan.png`): the primary logo with *Pearl of Satoshi* set under "bitcoin". Use on hero areas, posters, packaging and the first screen of a campaign, where there is room to read the slogan.
- **Horizontal wordmark** (`DeepaLogo_BnO.svg` for light grounds, `DeepaLogo_WnO.svg` for dark): the wordmark without the mask. Use in navigation bars, email footers, and anywhere the stacked logo would be smaller than its minimum size.
- **Wordmark with ₿ coin** (`Logo_b_BTC.png`, `Logo_w_BTC.png`): the horizontal wordmark led by the Bitcoin coin. Use where the audience may not yet connect the name with Bitcoin: app-store listings, ads, partner pages.
- **The Yaka symbol**: the Yaka mask is the brand's symbol. Use it alone wherever the brand needs a mark without words: social avatars, merchandise, watermarks, large crops on posters and packaging. `Yaka.svg` is the gradient version for white or black grounds; `Yaka_Black.png` and `Yaka_White.png` are single colour for one-colour print, engraving and embossing. Minimum size 48px / 15mm.
- **Bold Yaka** (`Yaka_Bold.svg`, and a heavier `Yaka_ExtraBold.svg` for embroidery): the same mask with heavier lines so detail holds when small or roughly reproduced. Use below about 200px on screen and for embroidery, engraving, stamps, distant signage and small print; use the original line weight everywhere else. Never mix the two weights in one layout.
- **Half Yaka** (`Yaka_Half_Left.svg`, `Yaka_Half_Right.svg`): the mask cut down its centre line for edge crops on covers, banners, pull-ups and slides. Left-aligned is the right half of the mask, flush to a layout's left edge; right-aligned is the left half, flush to the right edge. Keep the cut exactly on the layout edge, never inside the frame.
- **Laser-eyed Yaka** (`Yaka_LaserEyes.png`, transparent): the gradient Yaka with the brand's red laser flare on both eyes (white-hot core, long thin cross rays, soft red halo). The flare sits low in each eye, in the lower part of the inner oval, not at its centre. The flare alone is `BD_Laser_Flare.png`. Dark grounds only (`brand-black`, `charcoal`); one per layout as a hero moment. Keep the lasers red and on both eyes of a full mask; edge crops showing one eye are fine.
- **App icon** (`05-app-icon/`): the දීප symbol is the app icon and favicon. At icon sizes the Yaka's fine lines would fill in.

### App icon

- The app icon is the දීප symbol: `bitcoin-orange` glyph on a `brand-white` tile. It is the only mark used without the Yaka, because the mask's fine lines fill in at icon sizes.
- `AppIcon_Light.png`: standard artwork, glyph about half the tile. Use for the App Store, Google Play, iOS, Android and PWA icons. It sits inside Android's adaptive-icon safe zone (inner 66%), so no mask crops it.
- `AppIcon_Small_Light.png`: larger glyph, tighter margins. Use at 48px and below: favicons, browser tabs, notification badges.
- Supply a square 1024px master with no transparency; the platform rounds the corners.
- Three appearances: light (orange glyph on `brand-white`, the default), dark (orange glyph on `charcoal`) for dark-mode home screens, and orange (white glyph on `bitcoin-orange`) for campaigns, event apps and sticker packs.
- Never: an orange glyph on orange, gradients, borders, the wordmark, the ₿ coin or the Yaka on the icon.

### Profile pictures

- Social profiles use the ₿ coin with the දීප symbol (`09-profile-picture/`): orange mark on `brand-black` (the default), orange mark on `brand-white`, or white mark on `bitcoin-orange`. The ₿ inside the coin is cut out to the ground colour.
- 1080px squares; the mark is 66% of the width, centred, so platform circle crops never touch it.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the "b" in "bitcoin". Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) enters that space.
- Stacked primary logo: at least 120px wide on screen, 30mm in print. Below that, switch to the horizontal wordmark.
- Horizontal wordmark: at least 96px wide on screen, 25mm in print. Below that, use the දීප symbol.

### Do not

- Stretch, skew, rotate or re-slant the logo.
- Change the colours, or swap which part of the wordmark is orange.
- Use the full-colour logo on `bitcoin-orange`, or the black version on dark grounds.
- Add shadows, outlines, glows or a box behind the mask.
- Move the mask beside the wordmark, or resize one part against the other.
- Separate "bitcoin" and දීප, or translate either word.

### "Powered by" lockup

For products and campaigns Bitcoin Deepa backs (the Lightning card, the book launch pull-up): "Powered By" in Nunito Sans, small and right-aligned above the horizontal wordmark (`DeepaLogo_WnO` / `DeepaLogo_BnO`). The backed product's name is the hero; the lockup sits at the foot.

## Colour

### Brand colours

- The primary colours have display names: `bitcoin-orange` is **Satoshi Orange** (#F8931A), `brand-black` **Genesis Black** (#000001), `brand-white` **Whitepaper White** (#FFFFFE). The grounds are `charcoal` **Cold Storage Charcoal** and `warm-white` **Paper Wallet Cream**. Use these names in copy and design files.
- `bitcoin-orange` is the brand. Give it the most visual weight: big fills, buttons, the coin, the logo's දීප.
- Text on a `bitcoin-orange` fill is `on-orange` (black). White text on orange fails contrast (2.3:1); do not use it.
- The `yaka-1` to `yaka-6` gradient belongs to the mask. Use it for one hero moment at most (a header band, a large numeral), never for text or small UI.
- `brand-black`, `brand-white`, `charcoal` and `warm-white` are for brand artwork, print and marketing: logos, profile pictures, the Lightning card, decks and documents.
- The four `lanka-*` colours have display names: `lanka-maroon` is **Perahera Maroon**, `lanka-teal` **Sinharaja Green**, `lanka-saffron` **Vesak Gold**, `lanka-orange` **Thambili Orange**. `lanka-maroon` doubles as the headline colour on `warm-white`; the other three stay inside the secondary pattern. Do not use `lanka-orange` where you mean `bitcoin-orange`.
- `orange-50` to `orange-900` are tints and shades of Satoshi Orange for interface states, charts and illustration. For orange text on white use 700 or darker.

### Light and dark themes

The app and websites use two complete theme sets, taken from the Bitcoin Deepa app designs. Every role token has a light and a dark value, so a screen switches theme by swapping the set.

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `surface` (screen) | #EEEFF3 | #0B0F14 |
| `surface-card` | #FFFFFE | #1B2027 |
| `surface-raised` (tab bar, tracks) | #F8F9FA | #0B0D10 |
| `surface-inverse` (selected chip, secondary button) | #1F2A36 | #1F2A36 |
| `ink` / `ink-muted` | #000001 / #64748B | #FFFFFE / #94A3B8 |
| `line` | #E2E8F0 | #334155 |
| `primary` / `on-primary` | Satoshi Orange / black | Satoshi Orange / black |
| `orange-text` | #9A4C00 | #F8931A |
| `success` / `warning` / `danger` | #1E7B4F / #8A5A00 / #D63B3B | #3DBE7A / #FFBE2B / #F45A5A |

- Cards (`surface-card`) sit on the screen (`surface`) with no border; rows inside a card are divided by `line`.
- The greys are the `slate-*` scale: cool, from the app. Brand artwork keeps Genesis Black and Whitepaper White.
- Received amounts are `success`, sent amounts `danger`, pending items `warning`. @handles and prices are `orange-text`.
- The light theme carries the black logo, the dark theme the white one.
- The app mock-ups use #FA7119 for prices and handles and #F45A5A for red on light cards; both are too faint on white for small text, so the tokens use `orange-text` and the darker `danger`.

### Buttons

- Solid fills only. No borders, strokes or outlines on any button, in either theme.
- Primary: `primary` fill, `on-primary` text. Secondary: `surface-inverse` fill, `on-inverse` text. Disabled: `disabled` fill, `on-disabled` text.
- Rounded squares at `radius-tile` (12px), never pills. Pills are for small badges and status chips.
- Focus: a 2px solid `focus-ring` outline with 2px offset on every interactive element, shown only on keyboard focus.

## Typography

- Headlines are set in **Ubuntu** (`display` family), bold. The hero `display` style is bold italic, echoing the slant of the "bitcoin" wordmark. Use italic for that one style only.
- Text is set in **Nunito Sans** (`body` family). The slogan artwork uses Nunito Sans Condensed Italic; when the slogan must be set live, use the `slogan` style.
- Sinhala text is set in **Noto Sans Sinhala** (`sinhala` family) with the `si-body` / `si-heading` styles. Give Sinhala more line height than Latin.
- All three faces are on Google Fonts. Ubuntu and Nunito Sans are referenced in the master Illustrator file.
- Write the name as **Bitcoin Deepa** in running text. The lowercase "bitcoin දීප" is how the logo is drawn, not how the name is typed.

## Primary pattern: ₿

A staggered field of ₿ marks in `bitcoin-orange` at about 10% opacity (`Primary_Bitcoin_Pattern_Tile.png`, master `Pattern/B_Pattern.png`, a seamless tile). It is the everyday background texture. It repeats on an even grid (₿ every 86 units across, 44 down, alternate rows shifted half a step); the original 490 × 206 tile does not divide into that grid and shows seams when tiled, so use a 172 × 88 tile. Keep it quiet behind logos and type. On the notebook it runs at full orange on black as a printed band rising from the bottom edge; that is the only full-strength use.

## Yaka in layouts

- Edge crop: the half Yaka (`Yaka_Half_Left.svg` / `Yaka_Half_Right.svg`) flush to a layout edge, optionally with a glowing red eye on dark grounds.
- Watermark: the Yaka very faint behind content on `warm-white`.
- Hero: the gradient Yaka large on `charcoal` or black.

## Secondary pattern: spiky tiles

The CeylonCash heritage pattern redrawn for Bitcoin Deepa: the same sequence and the same four Lanka colours (tall bars `lanka-maroon`, squares `lanka-teal`, small tiles alternating `lanka-orange` and `lanka-saffron`, alternate bands shifted half a step), but every tile has straight sides and corners that flare out into sharp, symmetrical spikes, echoing the Yaka's crown. Seamless 752 × 752 tile on `brand-white` or `charcoal`. Use for bands, borders, packaging, merchandise and event décor; keep logos and text off it or on a solid panel. Files: `Secondary_SpikyTiles_Light.svg`, `Secondary_SpikyTiles_Charcoal.svg`.


## Voice

- Plain and confident. Explain Bitcoin in everyday words: no jargon without a short explanation, no hype, no promises about price.
- Bilingual. Sinhala and English stand side by side as equals; when space allows, give both.
- Proud of the island. The mask, the flag colours and *Pearl of Satoshi* say this is Sri Lankan Bitcoin. Copy can say it too, without clichés.
