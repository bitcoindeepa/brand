The Yaka mask is the brand's symbol. `Yaka.svg`: the mask in the full gradient, for white or black grounds. `Yaka_Black.png` / `Yaka_White.png`: single-colour mask (ink `brand-black` / `brand-white`) for one-colour print, engraving and watermarks. Minimum 48px. `AppIcon_Light.png`: the දීප glyph in `bitcoin-orange` on white, the app icon and favicon. `Yaka_LaserEyes.png`: the gradient Yaka with the red laser flare on both eyes, transparent, for dark grounds only. `BD_Laser_Flare.png`: the flare alone, transparent. `Yaka_Bold.svg` / `Yaka_ExtraBold.svg`: the gradient Yaka with heavier lines, for small sizes, embroidery and engraving.
