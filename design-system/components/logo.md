The Bitcoin Deepa logo and which version goes on which ground.

## What you provide

- The ground colour. Pick the file from it: white or light → `BDLogo_Black.svg`; black or dark → `BDLogo_White.svg`; `bitcoin-orange` → `BD_OrangeBG_Black.png` or `BD_OrangeBG_White.png`.
- The space. Leave clear space equal to the height of the "b" in "bitcoin" on every side.
- Alt text: "Bitcoin Deepa".

## When to use which

- Stacked primary logo: default for everything at 120px wide or more.
- Horizontal wordmark (`DeepaLogo_BnO` / `DeepaLogo_WnO`): headers, nav bars, footers, and anywhere the stacked logo would fall under 120px.
- Wordmark with ₿ coin: ads and listings for people who don't yet know the name.
- Yaka symbol on its own: avatars, merchandise, watermarks (the brand symbol).
- දීප symbol (the දීප app icon): app icons and favicons.

## Do / don't

- Do use the SVG files from the asset groups, unaltered.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects.
- Don't put the full-colour logo on orange, or on the spiky-tile pattern.
- Don't retype "bitcoin දීප" in a font.
