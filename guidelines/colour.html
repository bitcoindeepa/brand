Primary

Three colours carry the brand

Named after Bitcoin. Use these on everything: the logo, the coin, the profile pictures and every layout's main fills.

Grounds

Two working backgrounds

Softer than pure black and white, for surfaces people spend time on: cards and product on charcoal, decks and documents on cream.

Yaka gradient

The flame in the mask

Six stops from Bitcoin orange to a red tip, running bottom-left to top-right across the Yaka. It belongs to the mask artwork. Outside it, use it for one hero moment at most, never for text or small UI.

Secondary · Lanka

The island's colours, kept to the pattern

From the Sri Lanka flag, renamed for places and festivals. They live in the secondary spiky-tile pattern. Perahera Maroon also sets headlines on Paper Wallet Cream.

Orange scale

Satoshi Orange, lighter and darker

Tints and shades for interface states, charts and illustration, built around Satoshi Orange at 500. Keep 500 for anything that stands for the brand.

Use 700 or darker for orange text on white; 600 and lighter fail body-text contrast on white. On black, 500 and lighter all pass. Step 50 and 100 work as quiet fills behind orange-led content.

Neutrals

Slate greys for the app

The greys from the Bitcoin Deepa app: a cool slate scale that runs from the light screen background to the dark one. They sit behind the orange and never compete with it.

Themes

Light theme and dark theme

Two complete sets taken from the Bitcoin Deepa app designs, for the app and websites. Every role has a value in each theme, so a screen switches by swapping the set. Buttons are solid fills, never outlines. The light theme carries the black logo; the dark theme carries the white one.

Ratios worked out from the values above. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and icons; AAA needs 7:1.

Rules

How the colours behave

  • Orange leads

    Give Satoshi Orange the most weight in a layout: big fills, buttons, the coin, the දීප.

  • Black text on orange

    Text on a Satoshi Orange fill is Genesis Black (9.2:1). Artwork can be white on orange; body text cannot.

  • Darker orange for text on white

    For links and orange text on white, use #9A4C00 (orange-text). It reads as the brand colour and passes AA.

  • Pick a ground, then the logo

    White and cream take the black logo; black and charcoal take the white logo; orange takes the one-colour logo only.

  • Never white text on orange

    It fails contrast at 2.3:1.

  • Never Thambili Orange for Satoshi Orange

    The two are close but not the same. Keep Thambili inside the pattern, and never beside Satoshi Orange in one block.

  • Never the gradient as a text colour

    The Yaka gradient is for the mask and one hero moment.

  • Never outline a button

    Buttons are solid fills: Satoshi Orange for the main action, the inverse slate for secondary ones. No borders or strokes on any button, in either theme.

  • Never a tinted black in brand artwork

    Logos, profile pictures and print use Genesis Black and Whitepaper White. The slate screen colours are for the app and websites only.
