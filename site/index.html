01The idea

Bitcoin, from the island

The identity joins three things: the global currency, the Sinhala language, and a guardian from Sri Lankan mask craft. Every part of the logo carries one of them.

bitcoin

Set in lowercase, bold and slanted forward. It names the product plainly, in the world's language for it.

දීප

Deepa, "island". Custom Sinhala lettering in Bitcoin orange. It puts Sri Lanka inside the name.

Pearl of Satoshi

The slogan. Sri Lanka has long been called the Pearl of the Indian Ocean; here the pearl belongs to Bitcoin's creator.

The Yaka mask is the brand's symbol. It is drawn as fine line art in a flame gradient, from amber at the base to red at the tips. It gives Bitcoin Deepa a face that no other Bitcoin company has, and wherever the brand needs a mark without words, the Yaka is that mark.

The Yaka is the symbol

The mask stands for the brand on its own: social avatars, merchandise, watermarks, large crops on posters and packaging. Use the gradient version on white or black, and the single-colour versions for one-colour print, engraving and embossing.

Two more ways the brand uses the mask: cropped off the edge of a layout, half in frame, and as a faint watermark behind content.

Original
Bold

Bold Yaka

The same mask with every line drawn heavier, so the detail in the crown and the hood rays holds up when the symbol is small or reproduced roughly.

  • Use it below about 200px on screen, and for embroidery, engraving, stamps, signage seen from a distance and small print.
  • Use the original line weight everywhere else; it is the primary symbol.
  • Same colours and gradient as the original; never mix the two weights in one layout.

Half Yaka

The mask cut down its centre line and set full height, so it fills a tall panel and bleeds off one edge: covers, banners, pull-ups and slide backgrounds. Use the gradient version on white or black; the cut side always sits flush on the panel edge.

  • Left-aligned: the right half of the mask, sitting on the left edge of a layout.
  • Right-aligned: the left half of the mask, sitting on the right edge, as on the LinkedIn covers.
  • Keep the cut exactly on the layout edge; never show the straight cut line inside the frame.

Laser-eyed Yaka

The Yaka with the brand's red laser flare on both eyes, used exactly as drawn: a white-hot core, long thin cross rays and a soft red halo. The flare sits low in each eye, in the lower part of the inner oval, not at its centre. It nods to the "laser eyes" of Bitcoin culture and gives the mask a living presence on dark layouts such as covers, banners and event screens.

  • Dark grounds only (Genesis Black or Cold Storage Charcoal). On white the red glow loses its light.
  • Use the artwork as supplied; keep the lasers red or light only one eye on a full mask. Edge crops that show a single eye are fine.
  • One laser-eyed Yaka per layout. It is a hero moment, not a pattern.

Standard icon

Standard artwork. The glyph fills about half the tile, leaving room for the masks phones apply. Use for the App Store, Google Play, iOS, Android and PWA icons.

Large-glyph icon

Large glyph, tighter margins. Use at 48px and below: favicons, browser tabs, notification badges, where every pixel of glyph counts.

Light, dark and orange

Light

Orange glyph on a white tile. The default icon, and the one for light-mode home screens and light browser tabs.

Dark

The same orange glyph on a Cold Storage Charcoal (#1D1E1D) tile, for dark-mode home screens (iOS 18 and later supports a separate dark icon) and dark browser tabs.

Orange

A white glyph on a Satoshi Orange (#F8931A) tile. Use it for campaigns, event apps, launchers and sticker packs, where the icon should read as Bitcoin orange at a glance.

Sizes

Masks and safe zone

The dashed circle is Android's adaptive-icon safe zone (the inner 66% of the tile). The standard glyph sits inside it, so no mask crops it. Supply a square 1024px master with no transparency; the phone or store rounds the corners.

Two colours, plain tile

An orange #F8931A glyph on #FFFFFE (light) or Cold Storage Charcoal #1D1E1D (dark), or a white glyph on a #F8931A tile (orange). Never an orange glyph on orange, and no gradients.

Nothing else on it

No wordmark, no ₿ coin, no Yaka, no badge or border. The app name appears under the icon on its own.

Why not the Yaka

At 32px the mask turns into a smudge. The Yaka stays the brand symbol everywhere there is room for it.

Centred, inside the circle

The mark is 66% of the square's width, centred across and sitting a little below the middle, so the circle crop most platforms apply never touches it. Upload the 1080px square as it is.

Only these three grounds

Black or white take the orange mark; orange takes the white mark only, because orange vanishes on orange. No photos, patterns or other colours behind it.

One version per platform

Keep a profile on the same version so people recognise it. The orange one is for limited periods, then switch back.

10Patterns

Primary pattern: ₿

A staggered field of ₿ marks in Bitcoin orange at about 10% strength. It is the brand's everyday background texture. It repeats on an even grid: ₿ marks every 86 units across and 44 down, each row shifted half a step, so the tile edges never show.

On Black
On Warm White

Keep it quiet so the logo and type sit on top without competing. The one exception is the notebook, where the ₿ marks run at full orange on black as a printed band that fades up the cover.

Secondary pattern: spiky tiles

The CeylonCash heritage pattern, redrawn for Bitcoin Deepa. Same sequence and the same four Lanka colours, but every tile has straight sides and corners that flare out into sharp spikes, echoing the points of the Yaka's crown.

On white
On Cold Storage Charcoal

Tall bars in Perahera Maroon, squares in Sinharaja Green, small tiles alternating Thambili Orange and Vesak Gold; every other band is shifted half a step. All tiles use the same spike size, so each shape is symmetrical, and the tile repeats without seams. Use it where the brand needs colour and energy: bands, borders, packaging, merchandise and event décor. Keep logos and text off it, or set them on a solid panel.

11Voice

How we sound

Plain

Explain Bitcoin in everyday words. Introduce a technical term only with a short explanation.

Bilingual

Sinhala and English stand side by side as equals. When space allows, give both.

Proud of the island

The mask, the flag colours and the slogan already say this is Sri Lankan Bitcoin. The copy can say it too, without clichés.

Write
  • Send Bitcoin to family in minutes.
  • Learn what a satoshi is, in Sinhala.
  • Bitcoin Deepa · Pearl of Satoshi
Avoid
  • Get rich with crypto!!!
  • Leverage our decentralised Web3 ecosystem.
  • BITCOIN DEEPA, BitcoinDeepa, bitcoin deepa
